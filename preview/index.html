Ask us something else
I’m not a tech person. Is this for me?

Yes. Tell us what’s working and what isn’t. We’ll explain the options in plain English, recommend what fits, and help you understand the setup. You don’t need to arrive knowing what software to ask for.

What makes it a “smart” website?

It does more than show your name and phone number. Depending on your agreed setup, it can connect to quote requests, booking, customer records, follow-up, or other useful systems. The point is to make the next step easier for you and your customers.

Do I have to buy every system?

No. That’s exactly what we’re trying to avoid. We discuss your priorities and recommend a focused combination. Your final scope, selected services, and any associated costs are agreed before work starts.

I already have a website. Can we start there?

Tell us what you have and what you’d like to change. We can review your existing setup during the conversation and explain whether improving it, replacing it, or connecting the right systems makes the most sense.

What do I pay, and when?

The website build is $2,000: a $1,000 deposit, then $1,000 upon delivery. Support is either $100 per month or $1,000 paid annually. Annual billing saves $200 compared with 12 monthly payments. We confirm your scope and the support billing start date in your agreement.

How long does it take, and what happens after?

Timing depends on the agreed work and the information needed from you. We confirm the project timeline before we start. After delivery, your support plan gives you ongoing help with the setup covered by your agreement.
